From first visit to first unit exported

No sales agent hands you over to a contractor. The engineer who surveys your roof is the one who signs off the design.

  1. Roof survey

    We measure shade, roof strength and your meter load. Free, about 45 minutes, no obligation.

  2. Design and quote

    You get a fixed quote with panel layout, expected yearly output and the subsidy amount you qualify for.

  3. Paperwork

    We file the KSEB net-metering application and the national rooftop subsidy portal request for you.

  4. Installation

    Most homes take two days on site. Wiring is neat, labelled and tested before we leave.

  5. Switch-on and care

    After the KSEB meter change, you track output on your phone. We clean and inspect twice a year for the first five years.

Questions we hear most

Does it work in the monsoon?

Yes, at lower output. Panels still generate on cloudy days. Over a full year, the dry months make up for it, and net metering banks the surplus with KSEB.

What happens during a power cut?

A standard grid-tied system switches off for safety. If you need power during outages, ask us about a battery backup design.

Will it damage my roof?

No. We use anchored or ballasted mounts depending on the roof, and seal every penetration. Workmanship is covered for 5 years.

How long does subsidy take?

It depends on the portal and your DISCOM, but is usually paid into your account some weeks after commissioning. We track it with you.
